# TestFlight 1.0 (11) — What to Test

This build fixes conflicting offline corrections, interrupted saves, and duplicate counts during older motion-history replay. Update both the iPhone app and its paired Watch app to this build before checking correction sync.

Offline corrections now travel as complete, replayable decisions. If both devices adjust the same summary while disconnected, they choose the same winning decision after reconnecting, including when one increases the count and the other reduces it. Added cigarettes have stable identities, and a losing correction no longer leaves extra additions or permanent deletions behind. Try different corrections on both offline devices, reconnect, and check that their counts agree after sync.

Reviews, cigarette history, and sync queues now use atomic on-device snapshots. Interrupted corrections and automatic detections resume their unfinished saves and delivery after relaunch. Manual logs and Undo save their companion-delivery intent together with the local change, so a restart cannot strand that sync request. Failed correction saves keep the sheet and selected count visible with an error and retry action. Failed manual saves retain notes, and a failed Undo remains available to retry. Check a correction, close and reopen the app, and confirm the saved count remains correct. Delayed original events must not restore a cigarette removed by a review.

On iPhone, Add missed cigarette now appears before the review list, so accumulated summaries cannot bury the logging action at large text sizes.

Older motion checks now preserve classifier progress when retrying missing intervals. A replay that begins midway through an unknown episode waits for verified continuity or an observed quiet boundary before counting another session. Missing or malformed samples cannot masquerade as a pause. Ambiguous coverage stays pending for reconciliation instead of inflating the total; later sessions with a proven boundary can still appear promptly. Detection thresholds are unchanged.

Known limitations: an older companion app cannot apply the new correction transactions, so delivery remains queued until both apps are updated. Historical corrections already made with earlier builds do not contain enough information to reconstruct every past conflict automatically. If earlier motion has expired or was never recorded, some ambiguous history cannot be recovered and totals may remain incomplete. Motion detection remains experimental; similar wrist movements can be misclassified and closely spaced cigarettes may merge. Real-device background timing, recognition accuracy, and battery use are not established by simulator tests. Hourly opt-in calibration batches and the existing dashboard remain available.
