# TestFlight 1.0 (17) — What to Test

The paperwork is getting clearer. "Today" is now "Cigarettes Today" and counts up; "Daily Budget" is now "Daily Limit Remaining" and counts down. Compact widgets show when you reach or exceed the limit, including how far over. "Your Week" becomes "Last 7 Days," and "Quick Log" becomes "Log a Cigarette."

Small widgets have clearer titles, the elapsed-time widget loses its unrelated progress ring, and gallery previews use consistent sample counts. Ten original Ciggy symbols bring cigarette-shaped chart bars, coins and other custom artwork to the apps, widgets, complications, Live Activity and Control Center. Gallery copy adds a paper trail, receipts and a line in the ash. Existing widget identifiers and app destinations are preserved.

Please check widgets and complications already installed before updating, then try the gallery names. Compare today's count with the remaining allowance below, at and above your daily limit. Check dark and tinted appearances, narrow layouts and small Watch complications for readable labels and icons. Tap each widget or control and confirm it opens the intended screen. "Log a Cigarette" opens the form; a cigarette is recorded only after you explicitly save.

Recognition and background-check timing are unchanged. Physical-device checks across Control Center placements and Watch complication families are still needed; not every face or layout has been verified.
