# TestFlight 1.0 (18) — What to Test

Less instruction manual. More Ciggy.

The iPhone and Watch home screens now focus on the tally, daily limit, logs and corrections. Motion, background checks, connection status and the optional heart-rate chart have moved to Settings → Tracking details. One short notice remains when recent activity is pending or tracking needs attention; tap it for details. Manual checking is available inside Tracking details.

Reports, Goals, logging screens, Settings and the Watch summary have a new dose of packaging parody: receipts, small print, paperwork and a retirement date for the lighter. Live Activity controls stay on Today with shorter copy. Corrections retain their count and time window without the technical explanation. Private motion calibration remains in Settings.

Please check Today, Reports, Goals and Settings on iPhone, then the Watch home screen and Last 7 Days. Open Tracking details from Settings and from a pending notice. Try adding and undoing a cigarette, correcting a detection summary and editing your daily limit. Check larger text, dark appearance and rotated layouts.

Recognition and background-check timing are unchanged. Recent Watch activity can still arrive later; this update cleans up how that is presented. The leaderboard is not included in this build.
