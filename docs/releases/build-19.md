# TestFlight 1.0 (19) — What to Test

Welcome to the Ashtray League. Highest cigarette count wins. Management has approved absolutely no prizes.

The new League tab brings global weekly standings, a cigarette-podium icon, the top 50, your own rank, and last week's results. Join under a generated Ciggy alias to publish your weekly totals. Equal counts share a rank; each edition starts Monday at 00:00 UTC.

Please try joining, switching weeks, syncing a count, and leaving the league. Add or correct a cigarette and check that the published total follows the saved history, including decreases. Check that another member's rank moves accordingly. Try an interrupted connection, reopen the app, and confirm saved standings remain readable and pending changes recover. Check large text, dark appearance, and rotation.

Joining is optional. Leaving deletes the public league profile and totals after confirmation from the service. Counts include manual logs and automatic estimates; they update as the iPhone receives and synchronizes saved history. Weekly boundaries use UTC, so a league week can differ from a local-calendar report. This release has one worldwide board; regional and friends-only boards are not included.
