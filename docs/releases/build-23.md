# TestFlight 1.0 (23) — What to Test

Ciggy could freeze on iPhone when opening the app while the system notification service was slow. The phone now opens while pending daily roasts are cleared in the background.

With daily roasts turned off, log one cigarette on Apple Watch. Close and reopen both apps, then check that the count increases once on both devices after they reconnect. On iPhone, log another cigarette and tap Undo. The count should return to its previous value and stay there after relaunch.

If you use daily roasts, turn them on and send a preview from Settings. With notification permission enabled, check that the preview arrives.
